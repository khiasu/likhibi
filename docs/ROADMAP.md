# LIKHIBI — Development Roadmap & Schedule

**Project Duration**: 6 Months (B.Tech Major Project Schedule)

| Phase | Activity | Target Duration | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase I** | Requirement Analysis, Literature Review & Arch Preparation | Month 1 | **IN PROGRESS (Current Phase)** |
| **Phase II** | Vocabulary Collection & Parallel Corpus Development | Month 2–3 | Planned |
| **Phase III** | Lexical Database Development (~20k Entries) & Preprocessing | Month 3–4 | Planned |
| **Phase IV** | Development of Contextual Word Prediction Engine | Month 4–5 | Planned |

## Key Research Milestones
