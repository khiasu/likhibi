## 1. Motivation
- Nagamese is widely used across Nagaland as a lingua franca but remains a low-resource language in Natural Language Processing (NLP).
- Limited computational resources restrict the development of language technologies.
- Foundational language resources are essential for building downstream applications such as contextual word prediction and machine translation.

## 2. Aim & Objectives
**Aim**: To develop foundational language resources for Nagamese through a structured lexical database, a curated bilingual corpus, and prototype NLP modules.

**Objectives**:
1. Develop a structured lexical database (~20,000 entries).
2. Build an English–Nagamese parallel corpus.
3. Develop prototype contextual word prediction (N-gram + Trie).
4. Develop prototype English–Nagamese machine translation (NMT).
5. Demonstrate the developed resources through an Android application.

## 3. Literature Review & Research Gap
- **Sinclair (1991)**: Corpus-based approaches for language analysis.
- **Atkins & Rundell (2008)**: Systematic lexicographic resource development.
- **Sennrich et al. (2016)**: Improved NMT for low-resource languages using subword units.
- **Vaswani et al. (2017)**: Transformer architecture for sequence modeling and MT.
- **Joshi et al. (2020)**: Taxonomy and resource availability challenges for low-resource languages in NLP.

**Key Observation**: The primary challenge is not the absence of NLP methodologies, but the limited availability of foundational language resources for Nagamese.

## 4. Problem Statement & Research Question
**Problem Statement**: Despite being widely used in everyday communication, Nagamese lacks the structured language resources required for developing and evaluating computational language technologies.

**Research Question**: How can foundational language resources be developed for Nagamese to support prototype language technologies such as contextual word prediction and machine translation?

## 5. Expected Outcomes
- Structured Nagamese lexical database (~20,000 validated entries).
- Curated English–Nagamese parallel corpus.
- Contextual word prediction prototype.
- English–Nagamese machine translation prototype.
- Android demonstration platform.
